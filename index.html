About

Travel storytelling with a commercial mindset.

I’m Mostafa, the UK-based creator behind MasryTraveller. I create short-form travel content built around relatable hooks, real experiences and visuals that make a destination or stay feel worth experiencing.

My work spans creator-led travel content, hotel and accommodation content, and brand-owned UGC for companies that need social creative they can use on their own channels.

I have created accommodation content with hotels and Airbnb properties in Thailand, and UGC for brands including Replit and YouArt.

For partnerships, I can work as a creator, a UGC producer, or combine both into one campaign.
